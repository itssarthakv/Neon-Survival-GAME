# 🎮 Neon Survival

**Survive the swarm. Collect the gems. Beat your best!**

Neon Survival is a fun little arcade game where your goal is to survive as long as possible. Dodge enemies, collect glowing gems, and challenge yourself to beat your highest score!

## ✨ Features

- 🌌 Neon-themed visuals
- 👾 Enemies that chase you
- 💎 Collect gems to earn bonus points
- 🔊 Sound effects with a mute option
- ⏱️ Survival timer and score tracking
- 🏆 Personal best saved on your device

## 🕹️ How to Play

- **W / A / S / D** or **Arrow Keys** — Move your player
- Avoid the enemies
- Collect cyan gems to earn bonus points
- Survive as long as you can!
